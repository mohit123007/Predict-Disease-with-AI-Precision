🌟 General Wellness Tips

🥗

Balanced Diet

Eat 5 servings of fruits & vegetables daily. Limit processed foods and refined sugar.

🏃

Regular Exercise

150+ minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week. Strength training 2x/week.

💧

Hydration

Drink 2–3 litres of water daily. More if physically active or in warm climate.

😴

Quality Sleep

Adults need 7–9 hours nightly. Consistent sleep schedule improves overall health.

🧘

Stress Management

Practice mindfulness, yoga, or deep breathing. Chronic stress raises disease risk.
